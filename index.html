Our story

Three generations on Main Street.

George's AG Super Value has been feeding Enfield since 1965. We're still family owned, still on Main Street, and still run the way a grocery store should be — you'll get greeted at the register and the butcher will cut your roast the way you like it.

Being an Associated Grocers store means we buy with hundreds of other independent markets, so you get the brands and the prices of a big chain while shopping somewhere that actually knows your name.
