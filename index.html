Our story

Sixty years on Main Street

George's has fed Enfield since 1965. Still family owned, still on Main Street, still the kind of store where the butcher cuts your roast the way you asked and somebody knows your name at the register.

Buying through Associated Grocers puts us in with hundreds of other independent markets, so the brands and the prices keep up with the chains — without any of this belonging to one.
