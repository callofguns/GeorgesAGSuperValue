Foundations

The circular, made native

The store's paper flyer is the design language: mint stock, black ink, fat prices, and the dotted leader line that runs from a product name to its price. The interaction model is native mobile — 44px targets, a tab bar, a sheet you can drag away.

Everything on this page is rendered by the real styles.css. If a component changes on the site, it changes here.

Colour

Two themes, both first class. Dark is the default because that is what the store asked for; light is the flyer's own paper. Every pairing below meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) for body text.

Semantic tokens

Primary ramp

Colour never carries meaning on its own. The Blockbuster flag pairs amber with a ★ and the word itself; the open/closed dot pairs colour with text; an added item shows a check, not just a green fill.

Micro-interactions

Every one is defined the same way, runs on transform or opacity, and is switched off by prefers-reduced-motion.

InteractionTrigger → FeedbackDuration / curve
Press Touchdown on any control → scales to 0.965 (0.88 for round buttons) 120ms --ease-spring
Add to list Tap + → icon becomes a check, button fills, badge springs, 12ms haptic, toast with Undo 180–240ms
Filter Tap a department → the pill slides to it, grid re-renders, count announced 240ms --ease-out
Sheet Open → rises from the bottom edge; drag → follows the finger, scrim fades with it 300ms --ease-out
Swipe a row Drag left → Remove is revealed at 88px; past 160px it commits Follows the finger, 240ms to settle
App bar Scroll past 40px → hairline appears, page title fades in as the brand fades out 240ms
Reveal Section enters the viewport → 10px rise and fade, once, then unobserved 420ms, staggered ≤180ms

Patterns deliberately left out. Pull-to-refresh: the page has nothing to fetch, so the gesture would be theatre. Skeleton screens: the content is local and paints immediately — a fake loading state would make the site feel slower, not faster. A floating action button: the primary action already lives in the tab bar, and two competing primaries is one too many.
